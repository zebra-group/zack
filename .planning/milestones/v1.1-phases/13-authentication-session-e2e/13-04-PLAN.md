<objective>
Prove invite-only enforcement through the real flow (AUTH-E2E-03): a genuinely non-invited email address never receives a session. Per lib/auth.ts's sendMagicLink callback, isEmailAllowed (a plain User-row existence check, lib/allowlist.ts) short-circuits BEFORE sendMagicLinkEmail — so for a non-existent User, Mailpit receives nothing at all. The correct proof is the ABSENCE of an email plus the ABSENCE of a session, never the POST response shape (which stays byte-identical/neutral by D-01 to avoid an account-existence side channel).

Purpose: Close AUTH-E2E-03 honoring D-01's neutral-response, invite-only contract.
Output: One spec file under the `auth` project.
</objective>
