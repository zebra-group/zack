## Phase Goal

**As a** team member, **I want to** open a link's detail page, edit its target URL and slug (with the same safety rules as creation), and delete it, **so that** I can maintain my links over time. (Phase 4 Success Criterion 2 + LINK-05/06/07, D-04.)

<objective>
Add the three link-by-id endpoints (detail, edit, delete) to the existing `linksRoute` factory, each protected by the IDOR guard: resolve the link, then authorize against its OWN domainId, treating not-found and forbidden identically as 404. Editing routes through the same `updateLink`/`validateLinkInput` core built in 04-02, so a slug/target-URL change obeys the identical reserved/collision/scheme rules as creation (D-04).

Purpose: Link routes have `:id` = the link, one join away from the domain — the single most likely place to forget the extra authorization hop (RESEARCH Pitfall 4). Making the IDOR guard and 404-for-both discipline explicit and test-proven closes the enumeration/cross-tenant read-write vector.
Output: GET/PATCH/DELETE `/api/links/:id` in `routes/links.ts` + the LINK-05/06/07 and IDOR test cases.
</objective>
