## Phase Goal

**As a** dashboard user, **I want to** create static and dynamic QR codes for my short links — with a centered logo, custom color and rounded-module styling, re-pointable dynamic targets that keep a printed code valid, and a visible scan count — **so that** I can print scannable codes that keep working even after I change where they point.

> Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 7 `**Goal:**` line into user-story shape (MVP framing).

<objective>
Ship the public dynamic-QR redirect handler (`GET /q/:code`) — the runtime that makes a printed dynamic QR keep working across remaps (QR-02/QR-03) and produces the scan counts (QR-07). It mirrors `routes/redirect.ts` structurally but resolves through a QrCode's current target, and critically REUSES the redirect engine's state machine and the Phase-6 click hook rather than duplicating either.

Purpose: Honor the target Link's password/expiry gate on every scan (a printed QR outlives its target's access window — Pitfall 4) and keep click recording to a single write path (Pitfall 3).
Output: A parameterized `recordClickHook`, `routes/qrRedirect.ts`, app.ts registration, and an integration suite including the headline remap-preserves-code negative test.
</objective>
