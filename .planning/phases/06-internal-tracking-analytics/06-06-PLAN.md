<objective>
Expose TRACK-01 in the dashboard: add the footer "Internes Tracking" toggle to `LinkFormModal.vue` (default ON in create, pre-filled in edit, D-15/Surface C1) so a link owner can turn tracking on/off as part of creating or editing a link, and surface tracking state in the Links table (`LinksView.vue`, Surface C2) — a right-aligned Klicks column reading `lifetimeClicks` and a neutral "Tracking aus" badge on links with tracking disabled.

The toggle form is identical to Phase 5's forwardQuery toggle shape (which itself was pattern-derived from this exact prototype tracking toggle — Phase 6 builds the original). The emitted `trackingEnabled` rides the existing `createLink`/`updateLink` API client unchanged (the input DTOs already carry the field from 06-02).

Purpose: a link owner can toggle internal tracking and see which links are tracked at a glance.
Output: the form toggle + the table column/badge, both tested.
</objective>
