<objective>
Fill Phase 5's `recordClickHook` D-17 seam in `routes/redirect.ts` with the real privacy-first click write, delivering the phase's anchor guarantee: **tracking off = literally zero rows** (TRACK-02), proven by direct DB row-count, not a display filter. When tracking is on, exactly one `ClickEvent` is written per successful 302 (country via 06-03's local GeoIP, referrer host, daily-rotating visitor hash, `source='link'`) and `Link.lifetimeClicks` is incremented atomically in the same `$transaction` (D-13). The whole hook body is wrapped so a GeoIP/DB hiccup can never break the redirect hot path.

Also lands D-12 retention: `pruneClickEvents` (deletes raw events older than `CLICK_RETENTION_DAYS`, leaving the pruning-resistant `lifetimeClicks` counter intact) and `pruneDailySalts` (drops salts older than ~2 days unconditionally — a privacy property, RESEARCH Open Question 2), wired into `server.ts` as a simple daily scheduler.

Purpose: clicks through a tracked link accumulate privately; clicks through an untracked link leave no trace.
Output: the filled click hook + retention job.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| visitor → redirect hot path | untrusted headers/IP feed the click write; the write must never break or slow the 302 |
| stored click data | raw events are a privacy liability if retained forever without salt rotation |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-ZERO | Information Disclosure / Integrity | recordClickHook | high | mitigate | Structural early-return on `!trackingEnabled` before any Prisma call — zero rows is not a filtered read (TRACK-02, verified by direct DB count) |
| T-06-HOTPATH | Denial of Service (availability) | recordClickHook | high | mitigate | Entire hook body wrapped in try/catch; a GeoIP/DB failure is logged and swallowed, the 302 is unaffected (forced-fault test) |
| T-06-XDAY2 | Information Disclosure | pruneDailySalts | medium | mitigate | Old salts pruned unconditionally (~2-day window) so cross-day re-identification stays impossible even when events are retained (Open Question 2) |
| T-06-DRIFT | Tampering (data integrity) | $transaction | low | mitigate | INSERT + increment batched atomically; retention never touches lifetimeClicks — no counter drift (D-13, Pitfall 5) |
| T-06-2WRITE | Tampering | codebase | medium | mitigate | Grep proves `clickEvent.create` has exactly one call site (single write path, D-09/D-17) |
</threat_model>
